# What Is Biochar? (SAMPLE DATA - original illustrative text, not authoritative)

Biochar is a charcoal-like solid made by heating biomass such as crop residues, wood chips or manure with little or no oxygen, a process called pyrolysis. Typical slow-pyrolysis temperatures range from about 350 to 700 degrees Celsius.

During pyrolysis, part of the carbon in the feedstock is converted into stable aromatic structures. Roughly 40 to 50 percent of the feedstock carbon commonly ends up in the biochar, while the rest leaves as gases and bio-oil that can be burned for energy.
