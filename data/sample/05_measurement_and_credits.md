# Measuring and Crediting Biochar Removals (SAMPLE DATA)

A biochar carbon removal credit typically represents one tonne of CO2 removed. To estimate removals, a project measures the dry mass of biochar produced, its organic carbon content, and applies a permanence factor for the share expected to remain after 100 years.

Project emissions, such as transport and energy used during pyrolysis, are subtracted from the gross removal to obtain the net removal that can be credited.
