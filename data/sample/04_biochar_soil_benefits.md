# Biochar Co-Benefits in Agriculture (SAMPLE DATA)

Because of its porous structure, biochar can increase soil water-holding capacity, especially in sandy soils. It can also raise the pH of acidic soils, acting like a mild liming agent.

Biochar provides habitat for soil microbes and can reduce nutrient leaching. Yield responses vary widely; benefits are usually largest on degraded, acidic or low-fertility soils and may be negligible on fertile soils.
