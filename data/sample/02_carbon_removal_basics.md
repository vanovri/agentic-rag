# Carbon Dioxide Removal Basics (SAMPLE DATA)

Carbon dioxide removal (CDR) means taking CO2 out of the atmosphere and storing it durably. It is different from emissions reduction, which only avoids adding new CO2.

Durability is the key quality metric. Removal methods are often grouped by expected storage time: short-lived storage (decades), such as soil carbon or forests, and long-lived storage (centuries to millennia), such as geological storage or mineralization.

To convert a mass of carbon into CO2-equivalent, multiply by 44/12, which is about 3.67, the ratio of the molecular weight of CO2 to the atomic weight of carbon.
